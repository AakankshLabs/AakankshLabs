# StudX — Student Marketplace

**Project type:** Student marketplace / digital product experience  
**Live project:** https://brand.site/studx  
**Portfolio owner:** AakankshLabs

## Overview

StudX is a student-focused marketplace concept designed around campus buying and selling. The experience aims to make it easier for students to discover useful products, browse listings, and interact with sellers in a campus-oriented environment.

This case study documents the project for portfolio purposes. The original source files are not currently available in this GitHub repository, and the live site could not be independently inspected during this update. The details below reflect the project scope and features previously developed for StudX; they should be checked against the current live version before being presented as a complete feature audit.

## Product scope

The original project scope included:

- Product catalogue with student-oriented listings such as pre-loved clothing, accessories, stationery, art prints, and gadgets.
- Product cards with pricing and product details.
- Wishlist and shopping cart interactions.
- Quantity selection and checkout flow prototype.
- Seller profiles, seller statistics, and verified or trending indicators.
- Product-specific chat interfaces with locally persistent chat history.
- Newsletter subscription and social links.
- Responsive, campus-inspired visual design and interaction details.

## Experience and design direction

The design direction explored a colorful campus atmosphere with a playful, retro-inspired interface, product discovery cues, and lightweight interactive elements.
