# StudX — Student Exchange Marketplace

**Project type:** Student marketplace concept  
**My contribution:** Product conceptualisation and no-code website development  
**Website builder:** Design.com  
**Live experience:** https://brand.site/studx  
**Portfolio:** AakankshLabs

## Overview

StudX is a campus-focused peer-to-peer marketplace concept built around a simple proposition: help students turn unused belongings into cash and find affordable pre-owned items within their student community.

The product experience uses a playful, irreverent campus-hustle voice and a bold black, red, and yellow visual identity. Its pages present the marketplace from both sides of the exchange: students listing items for sale and students browsing, reserving, and managing purchases.

## The problem and product idea

Students often have useful belongings they no longer need—textbooks, clothing, electronics, furniture, and accessories—while other students are looking for lower-cost alternatives. StudX frames this as a student-to-student exchange opportunity, with listings and communication organised around campus life.

The screenshots supplied for this case study show the following pages and interface elements:

### 1. Marketplace landing page
- Student Exchange Marketplace positioning and a prominent seller call to action.

### 2. Product discovery and listings
- A shop page displaying product cards with photographs, item names, prices, and short descriptions.
- Listings across categories such as books, electronics, furniture, clothing, and other student essentials.
- A “My Listings” view showing listing status, price, feedback access, and edit/remove or relist controls.

### 3. Seller experience
- A “Post an Item for Sale” interface with fields for a product photo, title, price, category, description, pickup location, and availability date.
- A Seller Syndicate dashboard showing example quick stats, active listings, orders in progress, messages, and wallet balance.
- Listing-management and message-summary sections.

### 4. Buyer experience
- A Buyers Syndicate dashboard with example order information, messages, reserved items, and reviews.
- Order tracking and reservation-management entry points.

### 5. Communication and account pages
- A messages view separating new, earlier, and sent messages, with reply and order-related actions shown in the interface.
- Student login and registration pages.
- A contact page and social-media links.

## Design direction

StudX uses a high-contrast black, red, and yellow palette, expressive display typography, playful item names, and informal campus language. The tone is intentionally energetic and student-centric rather than corporate.
